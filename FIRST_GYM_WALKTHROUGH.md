## Phase 7: Rustboro Gym - Roxanne Battle 🏆

### Gym Layout
- **Type**: Rock
- **Trainers**: 2 trainers before Roxanne
  - Youngster Josh (Geodude Lv10)
  - Youngster Tommy (Geodude Lv8, Geodude Lv8)

### Gym Leader Roxanne
**Roxanne's Team**:
- **Geodude** - Level 12 (Rock/Ground)
  - Moves: Tackle, Rock Throw, Defense Curl
- **Nosepass** - Level 15 (Rock) - **STRONG**
  - Moves: Tackle, Rock Throw, Harden, Rock Tomb

**Type Weaknesses**:
- Rock is **WEAK** to: Water, Grass, Fighting, Ground, Steel
- Rock is **STRONG** vs: Fire, Flying, Bug, Ice

**Battle Strategy**:

**If you have Torchic/Combusken**:
- **Wait until Level 16** - Combusken learns **Double Kick** (Fighting-type)
- Double Kick is **SUPER EFFECTIVE** vs Rock (4x damage!)
- Use Double Kick on both Geodude and Nosepass
- Easy victory!

**If you have Wingull/Pelipper**:
- Use **Water Gun** / **Wing Attack**
- Water is super effective vs Rock/Ground

**If you have Lotad/Lombre**:
- Use **Absorb** - Grass is super effective vs Rock/Ground
- Heals you while damaging enemy

**General Tips**:
- **Heal between battles** - Use Potions
- **Nosepass is tough** - High Defense, hits hard with Rock Tomb
- **Rock Tomb lowers Speed** - Your Pokemon gets slower
- **Bring 2-3 Pokemon** - Don't rely on just one

### Winning the Battle
**Rewards**:
- ✅ **Stone Badge** (your first gym badge!)
- ✅ **TM39 (Rock Tomb)** - Rock-type move
- ✅ **Pokemon up to Level 20** now obey you
- ✅ **Progress**: 1/8 badges toward Elite Four

**Status After Phase 7**: FIRST BADGE EARNED! 🎉
